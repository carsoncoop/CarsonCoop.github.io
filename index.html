EasyClip

Apr 2026 – Jun 2026

A real-time audio distortion plugin for electronic music production. It has four clipping algorithms (soft clip, hard clip, foldback, and downsample) for shaping your sound and adding harmonic character, plus drive, threshold, mix, and output controls and a live waveform display. Built with C++ and the JUCE framework.
